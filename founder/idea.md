# WARS

- What it is: an anime/manga/light-novel reading and community site (Next.js + Supabase) with Discord-style chat, reviews, and an XP system.
- Who it is for: **~1000 anime/manga/light-novel readers** — a real community, not a friends-only circle.
- What it sells, at what price: nothing yet. Free to users. No revenue model stated.
- Where and how: online. Users arrive from somewhere public (social posts, anime/Discord communities, search) — acquisition channel not yet specified. Content: Death Note (PDF, pre-split to images) and ORV (EPUB) to start.
- Budget and constraints: **changed 2026-10-20:** the original plan assumed ~10 users on 100% free tiers ($0). At 1000 users those tiers (Supabase Free: ~2 concurrent realtime connections, 1GB storage, 2GB egress, 7-day pause) do not hold; paid infrastructure or a VPS is required and someone funds it. Solo founder. Copyright risk accepted by founder (now at community scale, not private scale).
