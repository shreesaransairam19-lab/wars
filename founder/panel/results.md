## What would flip a no

- If it had all the series we actually read, not just two
- If most of the group actually moved onto it and kept using it for a few weeks, so it isn't just another ghost site.
- If you could show the series are licensed/official sources and the group actually moves over, I'd be in.
- If someone I already trust in the group ran it themselves and showed me it's not logging anything.
- If it was hosted by someone I actually knew and trusted, and I could see exactly what data was being collected
- If it had way more of the series my group actually reads, not just these two.
- If you could show me the files are licensed or officially sourced somehow, I'd actually try it — the remembers-where-you-stopped thing sounds useful.

Buyers say they would buy **1.0 times** in the first month on average.
